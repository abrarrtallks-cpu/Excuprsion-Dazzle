Our Story

Srinagar Born & Kashmir Proud

Excursion Dazzle Tour & Travel is based in Gassi Mohalla, Safa Kadal — a historic part of downtown Srinagar, right at the heart of Kashmir. Working from the valley itself, our team understands that most visitors are missing the real, authentic Kashmir experience when they book through agents who've never set foot here.

That’s why we built Excursion Dazzle Tour & Travel — to bridge the gap between curious travellers and the authentic beauty of Kashmir. With deep local knowledge, trusted driver networks, handpicked accommodations and a commitment to transparency, we've become a trusted name for Kashmir travel planning.

Today, Excursion Dazzle Tour & Travel serves travellers from across India and beyond — families, honeymooners, adventure seekers and solo explorers — all united by a desire to experience Kashmir the right way.
