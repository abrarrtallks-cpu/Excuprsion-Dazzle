Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Excursion Dazzle Tour & Travel ("we", "us", "our") operates the website excursiondazzle.in. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website or contact us regarding Kashmir tour packages, vehicle rentals or other travel services. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Our website is designed to keep data collection to a minimum:

  • Basic, non-identifying analytics such as pages visited and general browser/device information, used only to understand how our site is used.
  • Any details you choose to type into our Contact page enquiry form (name, phone, email, message) or share with us directly over a phone call or WhatsApp.

How Enquiries Are Handled

Every "Book on WhatsApp" or "Inquire" button on this website, and our Contact page enquiry form, opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled and sends it directly to our team at +91 97977 06312. We do not store enquiry or contact form submissions in any database — your enquiry only exists as a WhatsApp message between you and Excursion Dazzle Tour & Travel, in the same way any other WhatsApp conversation would.

Tour, Vehicle and Gallery Content

The tour packages, vehicle listings and photo gallery shown on this website are managed through our own internal admin system and stored to display accurate, up-to-date information to visitors. This content is publicly viewable business information and does not contain any personal data about our customers.

How We Use Your Information

Any information you share with us by phone, WhatsApp or our contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry, plan your itinerary, and provide the travel services you request — such as tour packages, vehicle rentals or booking assistance. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) to relay enquiries, and may embed a Google Maps view of our office location on the Contact page. These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, which govern any data processed on their platforms.

Cookies

Our website may use minimal, essential cookies to remember basic preferences and improve site performance. We do not use cookies for cross-site advertising or tracking.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information shared with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or WhatsApp is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults planning travel. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
